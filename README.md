## Hi there 👋

Hello! My name is Haojia Gao and I come from Beijing, China. I'm currently a master's student in Artificial Intelligence at [Tsinghua Shenzhen International Graduate School](https://www.sigs.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), [Tsinghua University](https://www.tsinghua.edu.cn/en/), which I joined in September 2026. My research focuses on Embodied Intelligence.

Before that, I received my bachelor's degree in Artificial Intelligence from the [Fan Gongxiu Honors College](https://fgx-hc.bjut.edu.cn/) of [Beijing University of Technology](https://english.bjut.edu.cn/) in June 2026.

Feel free to reach out and connect with me — I'm always open to interesting discussions and collaborations!
